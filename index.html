Data. Donation volumes are the rolling 7-day average by state and blood group from data.gov.my's blood_donations_state dataset. Perlis, W.P. Putrajaya and W.P. Labuan are not reported separately and use a scaled proxy of their neighbouring state. ICU and ventilator occupancy is an illustrative baseline modelled on published 2024 state bed-utilisation ranges, not a live feed.

Model. Days of stock are modelled: on-shelf inventory is assumed at 7 days of collections, and daily demand is anchored to each state's collection volume, scaled by ICU pressure and the ABO benchmark (O 44%, A 26%, B 23%, AB 7%). Treat outputs as planning signals, not clinical inventory. Open for the production pipeline.

How BLifeline is built for production

This page runs the same stock-flow model in your browser. The deployed system splits it into three layers: a Next.js frontend, a FastAPI microservice and a Python data science pipeline.
